Questions

A few things people ask.

Is there a contract?

No. Your first week is free, and if you continue it’s month-to-month. There’s no lock-in.

What do you need from me?

A short kickoff, your logo and usual tone, and a way to publish or approve posts — either access to the accounts, or a simple sign-off so you post them yourself. You stay in control. We’ll also need access, or a simple route, to reply to your real Google reviews and update the Google Business Profile.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Stop after the free week if you like, or cancel any time after that. There is no contract.

Who writes the posts?

Jonathan writes and checks every post and review reply. Nothing goes out with your name on it in the first week until you’ve approved it.
